Sheet Change Detector — compare two Google Sheets and see exactly what changed.

What it does

  • Compares two Google Sheets and shows exactly what changed
  • Detects added, removed and changed rows, with a side-by-side view
  • Flags blank and duplicate keys instead of pairing them silently
  • Exports a shareable report; your sheet data stays in your browser

How it works

1 Pick two ranges in Google Sheets
2 Compare and review what changed
3 Apply the changes you approve
