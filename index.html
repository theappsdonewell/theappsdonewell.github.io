What it does

  • Compares two ranges in a Google spreadsheet, or a saved snapshot against a live range
  • Shows cell-level changes: added, removed, value, or formula, in a side-by-side view
  • Writes a diff report tab you can share, highlights changes in the source tab, applies only approved changes
  • Saves comparison presets, keeps 50 comparisons per spreadsheet in history, runs scheduled comparisons
  • Everything runs in the browser; spreadsheet data is never sent to our servers (only Google Sheets API + payment provider)

How it works

  1. Select two ranges in a Google spreadsheet, or load a snapshot to compare
  2. Run the comparison — view added, removed, and changed cells
  3. Write a diff report tab and share it, or apply changes to the source

Privacy

Sheet Change Detector only runs in your browser. When you use it, your Google Sheets content is compared locally using Google’s APIs in your browser and is never sent to our servers. Comparison results, history, and your presets are stored in your browser (chrome.storage) until you delete them or uninstall the extension.
