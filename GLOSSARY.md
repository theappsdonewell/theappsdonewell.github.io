# Company website

The public language of The apps done well and the products it publishes.

## Language

**The apps done well**:
The umbrella brand for useful apps, starting with Chrome extensions. It is the publisher brand, not the name of one extension.
_Avoid_: The Apps Done Well, Sheet Change Detector as the company name

**Product**:
An independently named app offered under The apps done well. Each product has its own purpose and may have its own plans.
_Avoid_: A company-wide subscription implied by a product plan

**Sheet Change Detector**:
The Chrome-extension product for comparing spreadsheet values and formulas and reviewing their differences.
_Avoid_: SheetChangeDetector, the company

**Product family**:
Products that share a recognizable publisher identity while retaining their individual names and purposes.
_Avoid_: Fictional products presented as available

**Company homepage**:
The introduction to the publisher and its real products, rather than a landing page for only one product.

**Product page**:
The explanation of one product, including its purpose, demonstration, capabilities, and any product-specific plans.

**Plan**:
A product-specific offering such as Free or Pro. A plan for one product does not include future products unless explicitly stated.

**Install action**:
The action that takes a visitor to a product's verified official distribution destination.
